About Me

I'm a motivated and curious learner from Bhutan, currently pursuing a Diploma in Computer System and Network at Jigme Namgyel Engineering College (2025 – Present). I enjoy taking a problem apart, understanding how each piece works and then putting together a clear solution, whether that's a routing configuration, a Linux server or a web page.

My interests sit where networking, cybersecurity and web development meet. In class I work with Cisco Packet Tracer, BGP and ACLs, and Linux servers; outside class I practise cybersecurity through Capture-the-Flag challenges on CyLab Security Academy and TryHackMe.

Beyond technology, I care about sustainability and cultural awareness, and I bring resilience, humour and a collaborative spirit to every team I join.
